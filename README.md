# Leanology app feedback

This is the public issue tracker for the Leanology iPhone and iPad app.

[Report a bug or issue](https://github.com/Leanology/app-feedback/issues/new/choose) or [browse existing reports](https://github.com/Leanology/app-feedback/issues).

If you can, include the app version and build from Settings, your device model and iOS or iPadOS version, and what you expected to happen.

Reports and attachments posted here are public. Keep personal information, health data, account details and purchase receipts out of reports. For private account, purchase or security concerns, use [Leanology Help](https://leanology.cc/help).
